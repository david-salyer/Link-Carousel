# Link Carousel

###### :robot: Made by Clankers :robot:

---

#### A Link Carousel to keep track of links as you view them.


### What's it for?

**Need to go through a list of links?**
- Focus on one at a time
- Mark links as viewed as you progress
- View links in any order
- Download your updated progress


### How to Use

- Either Upload a .txt from your computer or paste the links manually.
- If loaded list contains visited links, carousel will start on first unclicked link.
- The top left shows what slide you are viewing.<br>
(The bottom bar also shows this viewing progression.)
- The top right shows how many of the links you have clicked.<br>
(The top bar also shows this clicked progression.)
- The bottom left will let you upload a new file.
- The bottom right will let you download your current progress.
- Custom link title is in the center.<br>
(There is also a fallback to show the domain when no title has been provided.)
- The leftmost button takes you to the first slide.<br>
(You may also use the up arrow.)
- The second button will take you to the previous slide.<br>
(You may also use the left arrow.)
- The center button will open the link for that slide.<br>
(You may also use the enter key or spacebar.)
- The fourth button will take you to the next slide.<br>
(You may also use the right arrow.)
- The rightmost button takes you to the last slide.<br>
(You may also use the down arrow.)
- Once a slide is clicked, the play button turns grey, but it can still be clicked.
- The undo button below the play button will revert the viewed status to unseen.
- The trash can button at the top will delete the slide from rotation.
- If you want to copy the link for the current slide, right click the play button.
